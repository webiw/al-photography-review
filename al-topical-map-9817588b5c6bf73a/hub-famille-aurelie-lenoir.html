The template you reuse for the next page

  1. Title: search phrase first, then your name
  2. A headline that sounds human
  3. Two sentences about you, first person
  4. What happens during a session, and how long
  5. The areas you cover, named
  6. Something you really lived on this kind of session
  7. Prices and a way to get in touch
  8. Three to five links to your own articles

Avoid what reads as generic: superlatives, agency phrasing, any sentence that could sit on any photographer website.
