The 5 highest-impact moves

PRIORITY 1

Build a Maternity hub

A whole service you offer has no page. "Maternity photo session" (260/mo) and "pregnancy photoshoot" (170/mo) sit at difficulty 2 with almost no local competition.

Why first: highest volume for the lowest effort on the whole site.
PRIORITY 1

Rewrite the Newborn hub

"Newborn photography dublin" is 170 searches/mo at difficulty 13, yet the best that keyword reaches is 39th and the page itself averages position 70. This is the biggest single ranking gap you have.

Why first: real demand, weak competitors, easy to beat.
PRIORITY 1

Merge the two Baby pages

Two near-identical URLs share 8,900+ impressions and compete against each other. Merging them concentrates the ranking signal on one strong page.

Why first: your largest audience, currently split in half.
PRIORITY 1

Unify the Family hub

Five pages (home included) fight over "family photographer dublin". One clear hub, with the others consolidated behind it, stops the internal competition.

Why first: your money keyword, diluted across 4 URLs.
PRIORITY 1

Create the In-home Newborn page

A blog post already ranks 10th for "in-home newborn session dublin" with no service page behind it. This is your natural edge over studio photographers.

Why first: difficulty 1-3, and it defines what makes you different.

Consolidate into one hub and point internal links to it. Leave /best-family-photographer/ in place, it is an advice article: deoptimise its title and link it to the hub instead. Repoint the /family-photography-dublin/ redirect, which today sends its signal to the pricing page.
